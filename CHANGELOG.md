## [1.0.0] — 2026-06-19

### Added
- Privacy-first expense and income tracking with browser localStorage
- Recurring transactions (weekly / monthly / yearly) with auto-generation
- Custom categories with icons, colors, and monthly budget limits
- Budget rollover toggle per expense category
- Interactive analytics dashboard (net flow, category breakdown, trend charts via Recharts)
- Multi-currency support: USD, EUR, INR, GBP with locale-correct formatting
- Dark mode, light mode, and high-contrast theme
- PWA manifest — installable on iOS and Android home screens
- Full offline capability — works with no internet connection
- Transaction tagging and notes
